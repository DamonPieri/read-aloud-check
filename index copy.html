Read-aloud check

Paste a passage and what a speech recognizer heard. The check aligns the two word by word, counts reading errors, calculates words correct per minute (WCPM), and flags substitutions that look like recognizer mistakes rather than reader mistakes.

    Key: omitted · substitutedheard · +inserted · highlight = sounds alike, check the recognizer first.
